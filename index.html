Design India Forward Forum

Reimagining how India designs, builds & transforms

India is building at extraordinary scale.

Highways are changing how cities connect. New commercial districts are changing where people work. Factories, housing, transport networks and public infrastructure are reshaping cities and creating entirely new ones.

But building more is only half the challenge.

What is Design India Forward?

An Autodesk India platform that brings together the people who design, engineer, build and commission India's physical future. This isn't simply a conversation about software. It's a conversation about what software makes possible.

India's next design revolution may not be about creating more ambitious buildings. It may be about creating a better way to build them.

Pune Edition

Why
Pune?

Between what it has been and what it wants to become.

Pune is a city constantly negotiating between what it has been and what it wants to become. Its cultural and architectural identity exists alongside expanding technology hubs, industrial corridors, new housing, transport infrastructure and a rapidly evolving metropolitan footprint.

Cities across India face the same question

How do you accommodate the future without losing what made a place work in the first place?

Technology will increasingly shape the answer. BIM is moving beyond 3D modelling. Cloud collaboration is changing how multidisciplinary teams work together. Digital twins are giving physical assets parallel digital lives. And AI and automation are beginning to influence how projects are imagined, designed and delivered.

The way forward

Technology alone doesn't create better cities or better buildings. People do.
